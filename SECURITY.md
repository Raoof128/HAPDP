# Security Policy

We take privacy and regulatory alignment seriously. Please review the following process before
reporting security issues.

## Supported versions

All commits on the `main` branch are supported. Older tags receive fixes on a best-effort basis when
the issue presents a material privacy risk.

## Reporting a vulnerability

1. Email `security@privacy-proxy.invalid` with a detailed description of the issue, impact, and
   reproduction steps. Encrypt communications when possible.
2. Expect an acknowledgement within 48 hours and a triage decision within five business days.
3. Please do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities until a patch has been released and users have
   had a reasonable time to update.

## Responsible disclosure expectations

- Avoid testing on systems you do not own or have permission to assess.
- Never access, modify, or store third-party patient data.
- Provide working proof-of-concept payloads and logs, but redact patient identifiers.

Thank you for helping to protect healthcare data.
